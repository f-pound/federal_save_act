# Hinges — which premises the outcomes turn on

A *hinge* is a premise that (a) is coupled to another premise in the adversarial audit, or (b) sits upstream of a final conclusion on one party's path only. Generated by `tools/amicus_pipeline.py hinges`.

## Coupled clusters (adversarial audit)

- **challenger**: `challenger-scenario-alternative-process-denied` ⇄ `semantic-b-discretionary-denial`
- **government**: `government-scenario-alternative-process-approved` ⇄ `semantic-a-mandatory-approval`
